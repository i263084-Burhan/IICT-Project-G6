Our Heritage

The House of Notes & Noir

— stylish, mysterious and memorable. —

Born from nocturnal ateliers and a reverence for rare botanicals, Notes & Noir composes scents that linger like an unspoken memory. Every extrait is blended in small batches using aged Indonesian oud, hand-harvested Damascus rose, and warm ambergris accords, then macerated in dark glass until each note reaches velvet harmony.

Designed for those who embrace shadow and light in equal measure, our flacons pair architectural obsidian glass with champagne-gold detailing — crafted to command attention from the very first mist to the final midnight trail.
